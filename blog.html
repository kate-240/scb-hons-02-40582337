    First Concert

    Some things you should know

    When it comes to concerts, here is what you should do:

    • Travel with a friend or family member
    • Plan the journey ahead of time
    • Keep important items hidden

    Travel with a friend or family member

    It’s safer and more fun!

    It is better to go with someone to a concert—either a family member or a friend—if you have multiple tickets. If you're going alone, message someone you trust with your location and the venue address.

    Plan the journey ahead of time

    Stay organized

    Knowing departure times and checking for public transport delays will make your trip smoother and stress-free.

    Keep important items hidden

    Keep valuables out of sight

    Hide valuables in small bags or inner pockets. Many venues allow only small bags (A4 size or smaller) to make security checks quicker.

    Tips

    Buy an RFID wallet/purse for card safety

    Bring a small bag even if you don’t think you’ll need it

    Add tickets to Apple/Google Wallet

    Eat beforehand

    Share your location with family/friends
